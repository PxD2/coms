# board

Five constraints. No portraits. Taste is a filter.

**Woz** — The bus is visible. If you cannot probe the PMOC lane, it is a shrine.

**Jobs** — One store. A JPEG and a tensor are the same kind of citizen. Subtract Files + Drive + VRAM until one object remains.

**Elon** — Copies are the bug. Bandwidth is the product. Rent a capability; do not clone the world into the accelerator.

**AMD** — Chiplets, open-enough ISA, fabric as memory. Infinity-class lanes are objects, not a vendor slide.

**NVIDIA** — The world is tensors. The OS must know a tensor from a JPEG. CUDA is a dialect, not the kernel.

SLI, Optane, WinFS, 32-bit Windows: promising, then starved. coms keeps the field — we do not pick their license.
